# Privacy Notice

**The live transcript is served only to devices on YOUR Wi-Fi.**

## What happens to your data

The lecture transcript is produced on your phone and served by your phone to the browser you open on the same Wi-Fi network.

Nothing is sent to the app maker or to any third party — no analytics, no ads, no trackers, no accounts.

When no session is running, nothing is served at all.

## Open source

This app is fully open source — every line it runs is public and auditable.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the updated version will ship inside the app.
