Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 July 2026. The privacy marketing claims on the homepage are backed, section by section, by the policy below.

Kenosis AI is a fully offline on-device AI app. It transcribes audio, runs a large language model, reads your documents aloud, and answers questions about your own files — all locally on your phone. This policy explains what data is and is not collected.

1. No data is collected or transmitted

Kenosis AI does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet access — it declares no INTERNET permission and makes no network requests to any server. Nothing you say, type, transcribe, read, or generate ever leaves your device.

2. All AI processing is on-device

Transcription (Whisper), the chat language model (Gemma4), document embeddings for retrieval (embeddinggemma), and text-to-speech (Kokoro) all run entirely on your device's CPU/GPU/NPU. No audio, text, or document content is sent anywhere for processing.

3. How the model files are delivered

The large AI model files are downloaded to your device through Google Play Asset Delivery, which is part of the Google Play Store. That download is handled by Google's infrastructure and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Kenosis AI itself has no network access and does not manage that download. After delivery, the model files live only in your device's private app storage.

4. Your content stays on your device

Documents, eBooks, PDFs, audio recordings, chat history, and anything you generate are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, backed up to the cloud, or shared with the developer or any third party.

5. Permissions used

Every permission it requests is explained in plain language inside the app, under About → App permissions.

You can also check the permissions yourself on this app's Google Play page: open the listing, scroll to "About this app", and tap "App permissions" (the "Data safety" section shows the same from the data side).

Its permissions and tracker count can also be independently audited with Exodus Privacy.

No advertising identifiers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

6. Children

Kenosis AI is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

7. Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, uninstalling the app removes all of your content and generated data immediately and permanently. There is no server-side data to delete.

8. Purchases

Kenosis has no accounts and no servers. Trials, subscriptions and lifetime licences are processed by Google Play: Google receives the information needed to process the payment under Google's privacy policy. We never receive your payment card details. If you email us (for example to request a licence code), we receive your email and use it only to reply.

9. Changes to this policy

If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional online features), this page will be updated with a new effective date and the app will clearly disclose any new permission or network use before you use it.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact: octoflash.sup@gmail.com.
